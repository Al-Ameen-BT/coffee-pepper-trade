Execution layer generic-admin; `other-txn.ts` is an alien transplant; print documents abandon the identity (Helvetica/#000/#ddd).

Deterministic scan (`impeccable detect`, exit 2): 15 findings, 2 rules — `overused-font` ×14 (Fraunces ×13 + Helvetica ×1 at print-bill.ts:38; valid observations, real problem is inline repetition), `cream-palette` ×1 (false positive in context — cream derives from coffee product identity). All other rules: zero findings. Browser overlays unavailable (no browser automation).
